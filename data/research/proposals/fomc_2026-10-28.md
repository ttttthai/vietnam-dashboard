## 5. Uncertainties and gaps

1. **Primary pages not read in full.** federalreserve.gov is blocked from the sandbox (DNS), so the statement and implementation note were verified only through search excerpts, which agree with each other and with CNBC, Fortune, Fox and Yahoo. Exact balance-sheet wording should be confirmed once the site is allowed.
2. **Balance sheet.** The excerpts show no change in reinvestment or reserve-management policy. Whether bill purchases resumed after the September pause is not stated.
3. **Month-end convention.** Oct-2026 = 4.0 assumes no action on 29–31 Oct. The safe alternative is to apply on 2 Nov.
4. **Minor press discrepancy:** some outlets describe the September move as the "first hike since 2023" and others as "first since July 2023". Both are consistent; it does not affect the data. One early report (roic.ai) did not name the dissenters, while the Fed statement and others did.
5. Research's `data/research/release_calendar.json` (entries for `fed_fomc` and the month-end FX row with "2026-10-29 (FOMC)") should be marked as observed. That file is not edited here.
